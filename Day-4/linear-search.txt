Linear search.
Iterate through a collection one element at a time.

O(N) notation.
Meaning again 100 data points 100 steps (thought it may be less ofc if found early.)

Disadvantage, Slow for large data steps

Advantages:
- Fast for searching through small to medium data sets
- Does not need to be sorted
- Useful for data structured that do not have random access (like a linked list)
