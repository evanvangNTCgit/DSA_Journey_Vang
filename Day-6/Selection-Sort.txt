In selection sort you are looking through the array and finding the 'minimum'
Until it is finally sorted.

9,1,3.

Go to one, realize that is smaller than 9 so 1 stored at the 'min' variable.
Go to three, it is bigger than 1 so nothing happens.
Now 9 gets stored in a temp variable, 1 (min) is set to the first index. now goes to the index 1 was at.
