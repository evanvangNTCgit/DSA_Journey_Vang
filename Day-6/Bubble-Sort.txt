Sorting algorithm that compares adjacent elements. 
To check and see if it is in order. 
Then the next.
If not in order make a sort.
This continues until the List is in desired order.


5,4,3
5 gets stored in a 'temp' variable then gets compared to 4.
Ok 5 is more than 4, move 4 up one. Then put 5 in its place (using temp var)
Next... 5 is more than 3, so move 3 up one. And put 5 in its place.

4,3,5.
4 is stored in 'temp'.
Compare 4 to 3... 4 is more than 3 so move 3 up one.
Than 4 is put in its place.
Compare 4 to 5, 5 is more than 4... So nothing happens.

Time complexity is O(N^2)
Meaning it should really only be used for small data sets.
